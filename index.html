Hi! I am a PhD researcher at the CASCADE Research Centre, supervised by Professor Timothy Jones. My research interests lie in computer systems and memory architecture, particularly in addressing performance bottlenecks through hardware–software co-design and by exposing more information across traditional abstraction boundaries.
